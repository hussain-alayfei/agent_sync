Event webhook

        

          
Every event (story on air, done, withdrawn, broadcast live or ended) is posted here as JSON, signed with HMAC-SHA256 in the x-zaid-signature header. Use it to relay the show into your own real-time channel: your backend, a Supabase Edge Function, a queue.

          

            

            

              

              
Check the signature before trusting an event.
